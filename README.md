This mod adds TrueGear's software support to the game Casualties Unkown 6.1 Demo.

Currently there is custom feedback to:
temperature, hot or cold;
heart rate;
eating/drinking;
hunger rumbles;
vommiting;
fractures; 
broken bones;
dislocations;
bleeding;
sudden muscle or skin health loss;
infections;
sudden pain spikes;
getting ragdolled or ragdolling yourself;
respiratory rate;
hemothorax;
radiation sickness;
And on-use items;

Each with their own effect and are based off where the feeling is coming from and how far away they are from the body itself.
